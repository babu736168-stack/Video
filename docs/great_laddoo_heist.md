# The Great Laddoo Heist

*An original cat-and-mouse slapstick cartoon set in an Indian home kitchen. All the characters are original.*

Video: `output/great_laddoo_heist.mp4`, about 2 min 7 s, 1280×720, with sound.
Renderer: `src/kitchen_comedy.py` (story, timing, camera, sound) and `src/kitchen_art.py` (characters and kitchen).

## The characters

| Character | Look | Personality |
|---|---|---|
| **Tubby Billa** | Big orange striped house cat, green eyes, white paws | Proud and greedy, and always sure his plan can't fail. Every plan lands on his own head |
| **Chhotu** | Tiny grey mouse in a red cap, living in a wall hole labelled "CHHOTU" | Small, fast and much smarter than Billa |
| **Sheru** | Big brown guard dog with a red collar | Sleeps on his blue mat. Do not wake him with a frying pan |
| **Dadi** | Grandmother in a green saree with a white bun, round glasses and a broom | Makes the best laddoos in the neighbourhood and swings a broom very fast |

## Scene by scene (story time)

| Time | Scene | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | 1. Dadi's famous laddoos | Dadi puts a plate of laddoos on the table: *"Nobody touches my laddoos! Especially YOU, Billa!"* Billa, asleep, mumbles *"Zzz... yes, Dadi..."* |
| 0:11 | 2. Chhotu smells something sweet | Chhotu peeks out of his hole (*"Sniff sniff... LADDOOS!"*), tiptoes across, climbs the table leg and takes one. Billa opens one eye: *"Hehehe... dinner AND dessert!"* |
| 0:23 | 3. The great chase | Billa pounces, Chhotu zips between his legs (**ZIP!**), and Billa dives head-first into the flour sack (**POOF!**). *"Ha ha! Look, a snow cat!"* In the chase that follows, Billa runs into the table leg (**BONK!**) and sees stars. Chhotu squeezes the laddoo into his hole (**POP!**) |
| 0:38 | 4. The mousetrap plan | Billa sets a cheese mousetrap and hides behind the sack with a frying pan. Chhotu sets the trap off with a fork (**SNAP!**), takes the cheese, then sneaks the trap onto Billa's tail (**SNAP!**). *"YEEEOOOWWW!!!"* Billa shoots up and ends up spinning on the ceiling fan |
| 0:56 | 5. Never wake a sleeping dog | Billa is flung off the fan (**CRASH!**). Chhotu hides behind sleeping Sheru: *"Nyah nyah!"* Billa swings the pan, Chhotu ducks, and the pan hits Sheru (**BONK!**). *"GRRRRRR!"* *"Uh... nice doggy?"* |
| 1:05 | 6. Kitchen chaos | Sheru chases Billa into a fight cloud (**BIFF! POW! BANG!**). Billa runs into the shelf and the steel pots fall on him (**CLANG! CLANG! BONGGG!**). He wobbles around blind with a pot on his head while Sheru and Chhotu laugh |
| 1:18 | 7. Dadi returns! | Billa grabs the whole plate: *"Mine! All mine! Hehe!"* Then Dadi appears: *"BILLAAA!!!"* *"It was the mouse! I swear!"* (Chhotu waves innocently.) The broom comes out (**THWACK! THWACK!**) and Billa runs off with Dadi behind him (**THWACK! YEOWW!**) |
| 1:30 | Final scene | Chhotu picks up a dropped laddoo and shares it with Sheru: *"Friends share laddoos!"* *"Woof! Thank you, Chhotu!"* Hearts float up, Chhotu winks, **THE END** |

The end card shows the moral: *"Greed gets you the broom... sharing gets you the laddoos!"*

## Sound
Everything is generated by code:
- Indian-style background music that switches between happy, sneaky and fast chase moods
- cartoon voice beeps for each character and a growl for Sheru
- the sound effects: zip, poof, bonk, snap, a cat's "yeow", clang, crash, thwack, the fight-cloud mayhem, and laughter
