# The Stylish Monkey and the Joker's Terrible Day

*A cinematic cartoon comedy set at a busy Indian railway station (the fictional "Mastipur Jn.", Platform 1).*

Video: `output/stylish_monkey_and_the_joker.mp4`, about 2 min 31 s, 1280×720, with sound.
Renderer: `src/railway_comedy.py` (story, timing, camera, sound) and `src/railway_art.py` (characters and set).

## The 5 characters

| Character | Look | Role in the story |
|---|---|---|
| **The Stylish Monkey** | Teal flower-print shirt, red shorts, black sunglasses, always holding a banana | King of the train roof. Watches calmly, then pulls the stone-for-phone switch |
| **The Joker** | Yellow feathered hat, oversized pink-rim sunglasses, yellow shirt, huge red bow tie, patchwork coat, striped trousers, red clown shoes | Struts in like a celebrity, mocks the monkey, and then everything goes wrong |
| **Birdie** | Small blue bird | The monkey's best friend. Launches the poop strike, dodges the phone, high-fives the monkey at the end |
| **The Egg Seller** | White topi cap, green vest, big moustache; his stall has a sign reading "ANDE / EGGS Rs 6" | Hit on the head by a piece of the broken phone, answers with three eggs |
| **The Bodybuilder** | Red "GYM" tank top, giant muscles | Hit by the egg-soaked hat, lifts the Joker overhead and throws him into the rubbish heap |

The platform also has seven background passengers. They dance in Scene 1, gasp at the big moments, and laugh at the Joker for the rest of the video.

## Sound
All the sound is generated by code:
- Indian-style background music (tabla, a drone and plucked melody notes) that changes mood for the sneaky, chaotic, sad and happy parts
- cartoon voice beeps for each character, monkey "ooh-ooh"s and bird chirps
- a train horn and a station chime
- crowd laughter and gasps
- the sound effects: splat, crack, thwack, whoosh-boom and the sad trombone

## Editing the story
Every line of dialogue, comic sound word, scene caption and camera move is a plain list at the top of `src/railway_comedy.py`: `BUBBLES`, `WORDS`, `CAPTIONS` and `CAMERA`. Change the text or timing and run `python3 railway_comedy.py` again.
