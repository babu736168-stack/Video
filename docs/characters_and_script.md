# The Jacket Must Go On: Characters and Script

*A fictional, good-natured cartoon parody. Not affiliated with or endorsed by NVIDIA or Jensen Huang.*

**Logline:** Ten minutes before the biggest keynote of the year, the CEO's legendary leather jacket runs off to "find itself". To get it back, he has to go to the place where it all started.

---

## The 5 characters

### 1. JENSEN: The Visionary CEO
- **Look:** Glasses, swept-back hair with silver streaks, all-black outfit. In the finale he wears the jacket.
- **Personality:** Calm, inspiring and endlessly optimistic. He can turn anything into a keynote.
- **Superpower:** Explains matrix multiplication until stadiums cheer.
- **Weakness:** Completely powerless without his leather jacket.
- **Voice:** Mid-pitch, steady and confident.

### 2. SIR LEATHERWORTH: The Legendary Leather Jacket
- **Look:** A shiny black leather jacket with a popped collar, white gloves, a gold monocle and a curly mustache.
- **Personality:** A dramatic, posh diva with a sensitive side. Thinks he is the real star.
- **Superpower:** Has more fans than most rock stars.
- **Weakness:** Dry clean only. Prone to existential crises.
- **Voice:** Deep and slow.

### 3. CHIP: The Tiny Super-GPU
- **Look:** A little square chip with gold pins and a green core that turns red when he overheats. Has stick legs and sweats when stressed.
- **Personality:** An eager, hyperactive sidekick who over-computes everything.
- **Superpower:** Ten trillion calculations per second.
- **Weakness:** Runs hot. Secretly just wants to play video games.
- **Voice:** High-pitched and fast.

### 4. DANNY: The Diner Waiter
- **Look:** Paper diner hat, bow tie, apron, a coffee pot and a notepad, with permanently sleepy eyes.
- **Personality:** Deadpan and unimpressed by trillion-dollar companies.
- **Superpower:** Has refilled the same coffee cup since 1993.
- **Weakness:** Still waiting for someone to order actual food.
- **Voice:** Low, slow and flat.

### 5. WENDY WALLSTREET: The Stock Analyst
- **Look:** Navy power suit, hair in a bun, pink glasses, red heels, and a phone that always shows a stock chart.
- **Personality:** Panics constantly and treats every rumour as an emergency.
- **Superpower:** Tracks every 0.01% move of the stock.
- **Weakness:** Faints at every 0.01% move of the stock.
- **Voice:** High and very fast.

---

## Script

**Running time:** about 2.5 minutes.

### Title card
"THE JACKET MUST GO ON". Then the five "Meet the Cast" cards.

### Scene 1: Backstage (the countdown clock reads "KEYNOTE IN 10:00")
*An empty hanger labelled JACKET hangs on the clothing rack.*

- **JENSEN:** Ten minutes to the keynote. Where is my jacket?
- **CHIP:** Bad news, boss. The jacket left a note!
- **NOTE:** *"Gone to find myself. – Sir Leatherworth"*
- **JENSEN:** He's been doing this ever since we hit a trillion dollars.
- *(Wendy bursts in. Whoosh.)*
- **WENDY:** EMERGENCY! The internet says the CEO was spotted in a CARDIGAN!
- **WENDY:** Stock down 0.01 percent! That's... a small country!! *(faints, BOING)*
- **CHIP:** Relax. I know exactly where he went.
- **JENSEN:** Where it all started... The diner! *(dun dun DUN)*

### Scene 2: City street at night (chase)
- **CHIP:** I'll compute the fastest route! Ten trillion calculations... GO! *(turns red, starts steaming)*
- *(The city's lights flicker and go out. CITY POWER: 3%.)*
- **JENSEN:** Chip! You're using the power of an entire city!
- **CHIP:** Totally worth it. Turn left at the moon.

### Scene 3: Danny's Diner, Booth #3 ("where it all began, 1993")
*Door bell rings. Sir Leatherworth is sitting in the booth with a coffee.*

- **DANNY:** Welcome back. Booth number 3... just like 1993?
- **SIR LEATHERWORTH:** I needed to remember where we came from.
- **SIR LEATHERWORTH:** Three founders. One big idea. Zero customers.
- **DANNY:** And one cup of coffee. For four hours.
- **CHIP:** Wait... you built a GPU empire on ONE coffee?
- **JENSEN:** Leatherworth, without you I'm just a guy explaining matrix multiplication.
- **SIR LEATHERWORTH:** ...Fine. But I want my own spotlight. And a fan club.
- **DANNY:** So... can I finally take your order?
- **JENSEN:** No time! To the keynote! *(the jacket flies onto Jensen)*

### Scene 4: The keynote stage
*Spotlight. The crowd goes wild.*

- **JENSEN:** Ladies and gentlemen... the more you buy...
- **CROWD:** ...THE MORE YOU SAVE!! *(huge cheer)*
- **WENDY:** Stock UP 8 percent!! I can't feel my spreadsheets! *(faints again)*
- **SIR LEATHERWORTH** *(talking from Jensen's chest)*: Thank you, thank you. I'm here all week. Dry clean only. *(ba-dum-tss)*
- *(Danny walks on stage with the bill.)*
- **DANNY:** Sir. Your coffee bill. Since 1993. With interest.
- *(The receipt drops in: Coffee $1.29, Refills $2,840.00, Interest $4,199,999,997,158.71. **TOTAL $4.2 TRILLION**.)*
- **JENSEN:** FOUR POINT TWO TRILLION DOLLARS?!
- **JENSEN:** ...Do you accept GPUs?
- **CHIP:** Wait. Am I the tip?! *(ta-da!)*

### End card
"THE END", the cast list, and the parody disclaimer.
